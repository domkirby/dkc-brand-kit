# Voice and content

Dom Kirby Creative builds fast, secure, static websites for B2B small businesses, with a specialty in MSPs (the founder ran an MSP for five years). The voice is plainspoken, warm and a little contrarian: it stands for people and honesty more than it argues against tactics.

## Person and tone
- First person plural to a second-person reader: "We start by looking at what you have."
- Confident, not loud. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype adjectives.
- Short declarative sentences, then one longer sentence that explains. Fragments are fine: "No bloat, no noise, no legacy code holding you back."

## Patterns
- **"Built for X. Not Y."** is the core rhetorical pattern, for heroes only:
  - Home: "Built for people. Not platforms."
  - MSP page: "Built for your MSP. Not a template."
  Don't let it spread into every section heading.
- Headings are sentence case and often end with a period (set in `color.green-deep`): "Business is simpler than the marketing makes it look."
- Processes are numbered two-digit stages: **01 Audit · 02 Redesign · 03 Simplify · 04 Manage**. Each description starts with "We…".
- CTAs are short and human: "Start a project", "See pricing", "Send it over".

## Vocabulary
- Use: simple, honest, plain, fast, secure, trust, transparency, human-led, actual ("your actual audience"), sharp.
- Avoid: cutting-edge, funnel, gimmick, syndicated content, canned thought leadership.

## Money
- State prices plainly, with units: "$500 one-time", "$1,500 one-time", "$49.95 per month". Total to launch: $2,000.
- Say why it's affordable without apologizing: "We use AI to move faster on the audit and the build — and we pass that savings straight to you… Lower cost, same craft, human-led where it counts."
- Scope limits are stated, overages are not priced publicly: "Bigger or more complex project? Let's talk — we'll scope it honestly before you commit to anything."
- "Yes, it's on the homepage. We believe in transparency."

## Real lines to model
- "B2B marketing has a bad habit: treating prospects like traffic instead of people."
- "It's people solving problems for other people."
- "We rewrite and tighten your copy around your actual brand and your actual audience — cutting the generic, keeping the you."
- "We keep it running… so your site stays sharp without becoming your job."

## Proof
- Founder credibility: ran an MSP for five years and tried the whole rolodex of MSP marketing tactics before switching to content built on real expertise.
- Case study: **Resilient IT** (Virginia, CMMC-focused MSP) — moved off syndicated content to a site that publishes under their own name (resilientit.us).
